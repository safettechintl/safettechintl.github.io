**Honesty note on "dofollow":** most B2B directories do not publish their link `rel` policy. Where it could not be confirmed from the platform's own pages, it is marked **Unverified** — check the live profile's link after it goes up. A profile link on a relevant, trusted directory is worth having even if it turns out nofollow (referral traffic + brand/entity signal); dofollow is a bonus, not the whole game.

## Europages non-EU registration check (2 Nov)

**Question:** Can a company registered outside Europe (specifically Pakistan) create a Europages company profile?

**Verdict: No — Europages is effectively Europe-only for new company profiles, free or paid. Drop it as a target for Safe-T TECH.**

### Evidence

1. **Europages' own published eligibility criteria (official reply, Trustpilot, ~June 2026).** In a public reply, the europages team listed the requirements a company must meet "to be listed on europages, whether they use a free company profile or a profile designed to increase their visibility":
   - B2B Focus — B2C-only businesses are not eligible.
   - **"European Presence: Companies must be legally established and operating within a European country."**
   - Online Visibility — the company must be verifiable via a Google search showing its website and a matching business address.
   - Legal Disclosure — the website must carry a valid legal notice/imprint with registration details.
   - Prohibited Content — no illegal drugs, weapons or adult content.
   Source: https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.europages.com (europages team reply to the 19 June 2026 review by "m. g").
2. **Matches our own form experience:** the new-company form's "Country of company registration" dropdown offers only European countries; "Pakistan" returns no result.
3. **No live counter-examples found.** Searches of europages.co.uk for companies located in Pakistan (Karachi/Lahore/Sialkot), India (Mumbai/Delhi) or China (Shenzhen) returned **no** company profile headquartered in any of those countries. Profiles that mention Asia are European-registered firms with Asian offices/operations (e.g. NOBLE-PAC GmbH, Germany, with a Shenzhen site; HNH Naturals Limited, UK, "UK and India-based"). Non-EU **European** countries are covered — e.g. PAUN PARTS, Belgrade, Serbia (https://www.europages.co.uk/PAUN-PARTS-AB/00000005507385-001.html) and many Türkiye-based manufacturers (Istanbul/Izmir/Bursa), Türkiye being one of Europages' European markets with its own country section (https://www.europages.co.uk/companies/turkey/turkey.html). Profile URL patterns: `europages.co.uk/<COMPANY-SLUG>/<numeric-id>-001.html` and the newer `europages.co.uk/en/company/<slug>-<id>`.
4. **Beware a false positive:** Europages auto-generates keyword category URLs such as https://www.europages.co.uk/companies/pakistan/manufacturer%20producer/cases.html — "pakistan" there is a search keyword, not a company location; the companies listed on that page are UK companies (e.g. Wire Belt Company Ltd). It is not evidence of Pakistani listings.
5. **Positioning corroboration:** Visable (the operator) describes europages and wlw as B2B marketplaces supporting European companies, and points to its global partnership with Alibaba.com as the route for visibility beyond Europe — i.e. non-European suppliers are deliberately routed elsewhere. Sources: https://www.europages.co.uk/en/company/visable-gmbh-342776 and https://www.visable.com/en_uk/b2b-marketplaces/europages.
6. **No separate non-EU registration route was found** — no help-centre article, FAQ or terms text offering registration for companies established outside Europe; the only published route is the standard supplier registration, gated by the criteria above. (Occasional anecdotes about non-European profiles existing appear to concern legacy or misregistered listings; Europages states the same criteria apply to all registrations.)

**Implication for the backlink plan:** remove Europages from the execution list for Safe-T TECH International (Pakistan-registered). The user account we created cannot be converted into a company profile. Prioritise the globally open targets instead (Kompass, TradeKey, go4WorldBusiness, TradeWheel).
